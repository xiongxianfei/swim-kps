# Swim source synthesis evidence selected references and limits

## Key takeaway

Source agreement can inform a starting reference; disagreement and task limits remain part of the knowledge.

## Summary

This selected synthesis compares official coaching descriptions with physical explanations and limited stroke research. It explains why the package chooses provisional references for arm action, kick, breathing and timing without asserting one universal technique or personal benefit.

**Refinement date:** 7 October 2026. This is a claim-oriented desk synthesis, not a systematic review or an efficacy trial of the staged Practices. It combines the existing reference records with a targeted external recheck. Records distinguish source contributions, source independence, authored inference and lack of access.

## 1 Breaststroke arm preparation and recovery
**Question:** What compact reference can I use without turning a cue into a universal geometric law?

[Swim England’s technique guide](references/swim-england-2018-improving-breaststroke-technique.md#surface-arm-preparation-and-recovery) describes outward/round preparation, an inward upper-chest action and forward recovery. [USMS breaststroke pull](references/us-masters-swimming-undated-breaststroke-pull.md#preparation-pulling-and-recovery) discusses hand/forearm orientation, compactness and the cost of returning forward. These are two coaching organizations, not two controlled efficacy trials. [Basic Swim England tips](references/swim-england-2016-tips-for-getting-breaststroke-right.md#basic-breaststroke-sequence) add a beginner summary but are not independent of the first Swim England family.

**Selected reference:** comfortable extension → outward hand/forearm preparation → inward action in front of the upper body → direct forward recovery. Some downward pitch is normal. The targeted error is an observed oversized or disruptive press, not all vertical motion.

**Authored inference:** a compact reference gives a bounded movement to observe while retaining a workable recovery. It does not establish one optimal width, elbow angle, depth or pressure distribution. Standing rehearsal teaches a sequence, not the hydrodynamics of free swimming.

## 2 Breaststroke kick preparation is shared trajectory wording differs
[Swim England kick tips](references/swim-england-2018-improving-breaststroke-kick.md#foot-preparation-and-kick-completion) emphasize heel recovery, outward foot preparation and backward circular action with a long finish. [USMS kick](references/us-masters-swimming-undated-breaststroke-kick.md#preparation-kick-direction-and-individual-width) emphasizes direct backward action and explicitly notes variation in usable kick width. The latter also uses performance-oriented maximal-effort and turnout wording that is not adopted for this beginner self-coaching task.

**Agreement:** preparation matters to the intended interaction; the action has to reconnect to a long return.  
**Disagreement:** rounded versus direct-backward instruction and recovery-speed emphasis. Coaching language can emphasize different parts of a three-dimensional movement. The sources do not settle an exact trajectory for this reader.

**Selected reference:** recover heels comfortably, prepare the feet within unforced range, act mainly backward with a comfortable inward finish and return toward length. A slower preparation can be a temporary check, not a fixed one-second rule. No compulsory shoulder-width knees, maximum turnout, explosive power quota or glute-only explanation is justified.

**What would change the choice:** credible observation that the reference creates pain, fails to change preparation or worsens whole-stroke control; stronger individual assessment or relevant comparative evidence. A wall hold removes travel, so it cannot verify propulsion by distance.

## 3 Freestyle hand entry and underwater path
[Swim England’s front-crawl page](references/swim-england-2018-improving-front-crawl-technique.md#technique-guidance-in-this-source) recommends thumb-first entry and an hourglass-like path. [USMS freestyle pull](references/us-masters-swimming-undated-freestyle-pull.md#entry-and-underwater-preparation) favors a relatively neutral/flat orientation with individual variation. The discrepancy is recorded, not turned into a claim that the sources agree.

**Selected starting reference:** fingertips forward/slightly down near the corresponding shoulder track, a comfortable preparation and backward-directed action, then unforced recovery with body roll. The staged guide does not require a thumb-first twist, an exact S path, an elbow height or a locked forward reach.

**Limit:** this is an authored, conservative reference among coaching alternatives—not a demonstrated winner for every swimmer, a joint-load measurement or a way to diagnose shoulder pain.

## 4 Breathing usable air not a fixed count or forced vacuum
[Swim England breathing guidance](references/swim-england-2018-front-crawl-breathing.md#exhalation-options-and-breath-timing) offers nose, mouth or combined exhalation and describes side-breath timing. [USMS breathing](references/us-masters-swimming-undated-freestyle-breathing.md#air-access-with-rotation-and-recovery) relates breathing to the stroke but also includes forced-expiration imagery, racing breath reduction and tasks this package does not use. Those parts are not implied endorsements.

[NHLBI physiology](references/nhlbi-2025-what-breathing-does-for-the-body.md#ventilation-and-pulmonary-gas-exchange) explains respiratory-muscle/chest-volume pressure changes. Exhaling hard to manufacture a vacuum is not the explanation taught here.

**Selected reference:** comfortable exhalation while immersed, enough opportunities for clear-mouth inhalation, and a return to support before urgency. A side breath can be explored with the body turn as the breathing-side arm moves toward recovery. Face return before same-side hand entry is a reference checkpoint, not a rigid timing law. Air access wins over compliance with a cue.

**Excluded:** mandatory 1s/3s breathing, every-third-stroke breathing, forced emptying, breath suppression, hyperventilation and underwater endurance. [CDC’s investigation](references/boyd-2015-dangerous-underwater-breath-holding.md#dangerous-underwater-breathing-behavior) informs the breath-hold exclusion, not a threshold for a supposedly safe exposure.

## 5 Timing body line and evidence transfer
The earlier [arm-kinematics record](references/gourgoulis-2022-breaststroke-arm-kinematics.md) concerns trained swimmers at different speeds. The earlier [head-position record](references/cortesi-2015-head-position-and-passive-drag.md) concerns passive underwater towing. Neither is a beginner stage-sequence trial. Their endpoints did not yield usable article text during this recheck; no new findings or numerical effects are reported from inaccessible text.

**Selected interpretation:** stage procedures use observable event relationships, not universal seconds, angles or a prescribed optimal rate. A change in felt smoothness or stroke count is not direct drag, force or energy measurement. Supported rehearsal, isolated movement and whole stroke are different tasks; transfer needs observation rather than assumption.

## 6 Support symptoms and why a stage may stop
[Red Cross](references/american-red-cross-undated-swimming-safety.md#supervision-companions-and-close-assistance) supports lifeguarded swimming, a companion and close help for inexperienced swimmers. [RNLI](references/rnli-undated-float-to-live.md#floating-and-individual-body-position) concerns floating for air/support, not mandatory prone posture. [NHS knee](references/nhs-undated-knee-pain.md#assessment-boundaries-for-knee-symptoms) and [shoulder](references/nhs-undated-shoulder-pain.md#assessment-boundaries-for-shoulder-symptoms) guidance delimit symptom escalation; neither proves the cause of the reader’s discomfort.

**Authored safety application:** each stage includes a known finish and fallback. Pain or unreliable recovery routes out of the comparison. A camera, companion or flotation aid cannot be assumed to supply competent close assistance.

## What was checked and what was not
Current access is recorded in [Source review](SOURCE-REVIEW.md). The public safety, physiology and coaching pages and selected NASA, OpenStax and Cochrane material were inspected. Two swimming research endpoints did not yield usable full article content; inherited extracts are qualified rather than presented as newly verified. Embedded demonstration videos were not independently analyzed. No swimmer was observed, no coach independently approved this package, and no self-coaching outcome was measured.

The 15-stage organization, six-field cards, decision gates, comparison plan and dependency tracking are authored KPS design. Agreement between linked sources does not validate those design choices. The staged Practice is provisional for an individual, even where a component fact is well supported.

## Updated access and exclusion review

The current passive-drag recheck was blocked by unusable PubMed content and a PMC browser challenge. Its prior extraction concerns passive towing in experienced swimmers, not active beginner breathing. The breaststroke arm-kinematics article supplied bibliographic search information but no usable full text in this recheck; its previous abstract extraction is retained as inherited evidence. Neither is newly verified in full text or supplies an exact beginner prescription.

The USMS freestyle-breathing guide includes racing-oriented reduced-breathing advice and a drill variant without an actual breath. Those variants are excluded here. The inspected CDC report, Red Cross safety guidance and dependable air access govern the safety boundary. This release does not claim a separate new erratum review.

Front-crawl entry remains a genuine coaching difference: Swim England specifies thumb-first entry; USMS describes a relatively flat orientation. The chosen comfortable starting reference is an authored selection, not a proven winner. Breaststroke kick descriptions also differ in path emphasis. No exact knee width, forced joint rotation or mandatory pause is inferred.

This recheck does not certify all content from any source or claim an exhaustive review. No new swimming session, injury diagnosis or personal outcome is asserted.
