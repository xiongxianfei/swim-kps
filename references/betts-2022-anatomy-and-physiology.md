# Anatomy and Physiology 2e

## Key takeaway

The selected textbook section explains cellular contributions to endurance adaptation.

## Summary

Endurance training can change mitochondrial and capillary supply in muscle, supporting sustained aerobic energy production. This is a general physiological account, not a swimming programme.
