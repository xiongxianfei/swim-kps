# Improving your breaststroke technique

## Key takeaway

This surface-stroke guide describes outward preparation, inward arm action, forward recovery and breathing/leg timing.

## Summary

## Surface arm preparation and recovery

The surface arm reference proceeds from extension through outward preparation and an inward action to forward recovery; it discourages an excessive sweep.

**Locator:** Arm action

## Breathing and whole stroke timing

The guidance coordinates breathing with shoulder/body rise and arm–leg timing.

**Locator:** Timing; Breathing

## What this source does not establish

Not a controlled trial or proof of an exact individual hand path. Its advanced timing, explosive exhalation and turn instructions are not mandatory self-coaching rules.
