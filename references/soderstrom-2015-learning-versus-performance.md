# Learning versus performance An integrative review

## Key takeaway

Performance during practice does not by itself establish lasting learning.

## Summary

The review distinguishes observable performance from relatively lasting learning and discusses retention and transfer. The local use is this distinction, not a specific feedback schedule.

## Source identity

Soderstrom, N. C., and Bjork, R. A. (2015). *Learning versus performance: An integrative review*. **Perspectives on Psychological Science**, 10(2), 176–199. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25910388/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000).
