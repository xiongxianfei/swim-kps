# Float to Live

## Key takeaway

Float to Live is emergency-oriented guidance for maintaining air access and regaining breathing control.

## Summary

Float to Live is emergency-oriented guidance for maintaining air access and regaining breathing control. It recognizes variation in floating. It is not a performance model or evidence that everyone should float perfectly horizontally.


## Current inspection and use limit

The official floating safety guidance was inspected. Self-coaching tasks retain their own support and stopping boundaries; the page is not evidence for a universal rigid body position.

## Floating and individual body position

Floating can involve gentle movement and varies between people; perfectly horizontal legs are not a universal condition.

**Locator:** Float to Live instructions and explanation

## What this source does not establish

Emergency floating guidance is not a diagnostic of swimming efficiency or permission to practise unsafe unsupported submersion.
