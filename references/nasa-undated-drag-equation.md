## Drag equation and flow dependent coefficient

The conventional drag equation relates drag to density, speed squared, reference area and a drag coefficient; the coefficient depends on the configuration and reference-area convention.

**Locator:** Drag equation; drag coefficient; reference area sections

## Reference area convention

Changing the chosen reference area changes the corresponding coefficient convention. The equation is not a measurement of an individual swimmer’s coefficient or energy use.

**Locator:** Reference area and drag-coefficient discussion.

## What this source does not establish

It does not give an active swimmer a constant coefficient, an exact arm path, or a measured metabolic-efficiency improvement.
