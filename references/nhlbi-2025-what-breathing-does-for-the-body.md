## Ventilation and pulmonary gas exchange

Ventilation brings air to the lungs and removes expired gas; pulmonary gas exchange transfers oxygen and carbon dioxide between air and blood.

**Locator:** Breathing in; gas exchange; breathing out

## What this source does not establish

Does not prescribe nose-only exhalation, fixed seconds, stroke counts or treatment for exercise breathlessness.
