# Fatal and Nonfatal Drowning Outcomes Related to Dangerous Underwater Breath Holding Behaviors New York State 1988 2011

## Key takeaway

This is an observational review of drowning incidents associated with dangerous underwater breath-holding behaviours.

## Summary

This is an observational review of drowning incidents associated with dangerous underwater breath-holding behaviours. It supports excluding breath-restriction challenges from self-coaching. It does not calculate an individual’s risk or identify a safe number of underwater seconds.


## Current inspection and use limit

The CDC report on dangerous underwater breath-holding behaviors was inspected. It supplies a safety exclusion, not a personal risk estimate or a breathing-training protocol.

## Dangerous underwater breathing behavior

The case review describes drowning incidents associated with dangerous breath-holding behaviours, including intentional hyperventilation.

**Locator:** Opening report text; discussion

## What this source does not establish

It does not give an individual risk estimate or a safe breath-hold threshold.

Do not read the report as permission for a “safe” duration. Hyperventilation before submersion and prolonged breath-holding are not techniques in this package. The observed case series is not an individual risk calculator.
