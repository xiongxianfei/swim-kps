## Technique guidance in this source

The guide describes alternating arm action with kick and rotation; it also recommends thumb-first entry and an hourglass-like hand path.

**Locator:** Arm action; Kicking; Breathing

## Entry recommendations differ between guides

Thumb-first entry and a shaped hand path are recommendations in this guide. They differ from the relatively flat entry described in the USMS guide.

**Locator:** Arm action section.

## What this source does not establish

The particular entry/path recommendations differ from the USMS starting reference. This record preserves the disagreement rather than treating either as a demonstrated universal optimum.
