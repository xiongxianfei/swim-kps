# Improve your breathing in front crawl

## Key takeaway

The coaching page permits underwater exhalation through the nose, mouth or both and discusses side breathing with the stroke.

## Summary

The coaching page permits underwater exhalation through the nose, mouth or both and discusses side breathing with the stroke. A common rhythm is an option, not a requirement to tolerate breathlessness.
