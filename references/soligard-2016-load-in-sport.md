# How much is too much Part 1 Load in sport and risk of injury

## Key takeaway

External workload and an individual's response to it are different quantities.

## Summary

The consensus distinguishes external load from internal response and describes individual variation and the interaction of loading and recovery. These distinctions inform the local qualitative account; numerical risk rules are not adopted.

## Source identity

Soligard and coauthors (2016), *How much is too much? (Part 1) International Olympic Committee consensus statement on load in sport and risk of injury*. **British Journal of Sports Medicine**, 50(17), 1030–1041. ## Limits and nonclaims

This is a 2016 sport consensus with selected access, not a current personalised guideline or a beginner-swimming trial. It establishes no safe workload ratio, percentage increase, recovery interval or injury diagnosis here. Citation to the consensus does not count as independent inspection of its cited studies.
