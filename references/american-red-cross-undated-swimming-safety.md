## Key takeaway

This is public water-safety guidance about supervision, companions, skills and assistance.

## Summary

This is public water-safety guidance about supervision, companions, skills and assistance. It defines the boundary for self-directed pool practice. A written knowledge base does not replace the support or competence needed to enter the water safely.


## Current inspection and use limit
