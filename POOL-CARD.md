# Poolside reminder after reading the chosen stage

## Key takeaway

One safe question, one adjustment, one honest observation.

## Summary

This compact reminder is for a task whose movement and safety prerequisites are already understood. It is not instruction for entering water, learning recovery or diagnosing symptoms.

**Safety before the cue:** Lifeguarded pool, companion and suitable close assistance; known stopping/recovery. Stop for pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness, panic or failed air access. Never use breath restriction, forced joint range or a painful control. A board is not a safety device.

## Do
Use the stage’s specified setup and movement. Choose one cue, ordinary breathing/rest and two safe options. Do not exaggerate an error. An unsafe usual version is not a control to repeat.

## Observe
Did the intended movement occur? Was air access comfortable? Did the adjustment survive a safe whole-stroke check? Note task/order/effort differences. Sensation, count or appearance alone does not measure drag or prove cause.

## Decide
Keep provisionally / modify / discard / unresolved / get help. Record one observation and the next question; stop rather than chasing a promised distance.

**Breaststroke:** B1 air → B2 support → B3 kick → B4 kick/breath bridge → B5 arms → B6 arms/breath → B7 whole stroke → B8 continuity/demand.  
**Freestyle:** F1 air → F2 body line → F3 kick → F4 arms → F5 side breath → F6 whole stroke → F7 demand.
