# Record a result and update working technique

## Key takeaway

Keep experience useful without promoting a personal cue or one good repeat into universal fact.

## Summary

A record can preserve the difference between what was intended, what happened and what was inferred. The current technique can change while the observation stays intact. Record the actual attempt, not the hoped-for result. Keep a cue provisionally only with its observed effect and context; leave untested references labelled “reference to try.”

## Inputs and prerequisites

A concrete question, the material or actual record to inspect, and enough context to distinguish observed information from a proposed interpretation. No result is assumed in advance.

## Local explanatory basis

A sensation, visible motion or single performance change can be consistent with more than one explanation.

## Objective
Keep experience useful without promoting a personal cue or one good repeat into universal fact.

## Rationale
A record can preserve the difference between what was intended, what happened and what was inferred. The current technique can change while the observation stays intact.

## Evidence and reasoning
[Self-coaching hypothesis and decision model](../models/self-coaching-model.md) supplies the decision structure. This is an authored recording convention, not a claim that logging alone improves motor learning.

## Steps
1. After an informative actual session, note the Practice version and stage ID, plus the Method used when relevant. Do not create a fictional filled example.
2. Separate the question, intended change, actual execution, observation and interpretation. Record any order, fatigue, support, effort or push-off difference.
3. Choose keep provisionally, modify, discard, unresolved or get help; explain what evidence motivated the decision.
4. Update the single Current Practice Summary inside the appropriate stroke file. Leave its opening Summary as a link to that state rather than a second maintained copy.
5. Link the current action/cue to a real record when one exists. A cited reference is not evidence of personal benefit.
6. Name one useful next check. Keep records private by default and stop retaining cues that make the whole stroke worse.

## Expected effect
A traceable personal decision with uncertainty retained.

## Validation and counterevidence
An update needs an actual supporting observation, not merely a citation to coaching guidance. “No conclusion” is a valid outcome.

## Limits
Records may contain sensitive health/performance information. Keep private by default; do not fabricate completion or diagnosed mechanisms.

## Template
[Swim session record template](../SESSION-TEMPLATE.md) is intentionally blank. Create a local records subfolder only when a real record exists.

## Local evidence summary

This operational account is an authored synthesis. Its immediate reasoning is stated here; related movement references and scientific explanations support particular components, not an independently tested self-coaching programme.

## Deeper knowledge

Canonical dependencies and Reference records provide additional derivation, source inspection and alternatives. They are not required to carry out the local explanation or procedure.
