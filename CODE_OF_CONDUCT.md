# Swim KPS code of conduct

## Key takeaway
Treat contributors and swimmers respectfully and separate reasoned disagreement from personal criticism.

## Summary
Treat contributors and swimmers respectfully and separate reasoned disagreement from personal criticism.

## Expected conduct
Critique claims and methods rather than people's bodies, technique, confidence or abilities. Be receptive to evidence, limitations and uncertainty. Do not ridicule inexperienced swimmers or pressure anyone to attempt a maneuver.

## Unacceptable conduct
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publication of identifiable session records, doxxing, repeated hostile disruption and requests for unnecessary private health details are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting
This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and official community spaces. Do not use public issues for identifying or sensitive reports. The owner must configure a real confidential maintainer contact before relying on private misconduct reports; GitHub's platform abuse controls remain available meanwhile.
