# Drag

## Key takeaway

Drag concerns fluid force opposing relative motion; effort and stroke count are not drag measurements.

## Summary

Drag is a component of fluid force opposing specified relative motion. Its magnitude depends on flow and configuration. Active swimming is unsteady, so separating propulsion and resistance is a useful approximation, not a complete measurement of every fluid force.

## Definition and use

The component of fluid force opposing relative movement through water.

Raising frontal area may affect resistance, but a slower repeat could also reflect less effort, different timing or a different push-off. A towing experiment measures a constrained passive condition, not a complete active stroke.

## Boundary and non example

A general drag equation needs appropriate coefficients and reference area. It cannot turn a phone video into a drag measurement or specify one ideal hand path.
