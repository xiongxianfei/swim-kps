# Coordination

## Key takeaway

Coordination is the timing between actions, not just whether each action looks correct alone.

## Summary

Coordination describes relationships among arm action, legs, breathing and body movement through time. In breaststroke, recovery and propulsive events interact. In front crawl, alternating arms, kick and the breathing turn must coexist at a manageable demand.

## Definition and use

The timing and interaction of components as they accomplish a whole-stroke task.

“The kick begins while the hands are returning” describes an event relationship. “My timing is bad” does not. Choose two visible events and inspect their order rather than prescribing a universal interval.

## Boundary and non example

A mnemonic groups attention; it need not divide a moving stroke into pauses. A drill can change coordination by removing support, balance or breathing demands.
