# Breathing cycle

## Key takeaway

Breathing involves air movement and an opportunity to inhale—not a compulsory count of seconds.

## Summary

A breathing cycle includes inhalation and exhalation. During surface swimming, inhalation requires the mouth to be clear of water, while exhalation can occur during immersion. The stroke must leave adequate opportunity for breathing at the selected effort.

## Definition and use

The sequence of inhalation and exhalation integrated with opportunities for air access.

In breaststroke, observe inhalation relative to inward arm action and return forward. In front crawl, observe the side turn relative to the recovery arm. Comfortable nose, mouth or combined exhalation are options; a fixed 1:3-second ratio is not a universal requirement.

## Boundary and non example

Do not turn this concept into a breath-hold, hyperventilation or underwater-distance exercise. Breathlessness can involve effort or health factors and is not diagnosed from timing alone.
