# Limb orientation

## Key takeaway

Which way a hand, forearm or foot faces changes the interaction being attempted.

## Summary

Limb orientation describes the direction of a surface relative to the body and local water motion. Orientation, movement and flow act together; the same effort with a different setup need not produce the same effect.

## Definition and use

The position and direction of a hand, forearm, lower leg or foot relative to body and water.

Breaststroke foot preparation occurs before the main kick; the front-crawl catch repositions the hand and forearm before much of the underwater action. Observe preparation without assuming a single exact angle for everyone.

## Boundary and non example

A photograph cannot reveal the full local flow or force. Never force knee, ankle, hip or shoulder range to imitate an idealized propulsive surface.
