# Observation and hypothesis

## Key takeaway

A felt or visible event is data; its suspected cause is a separate hypothesis.

## Summary

An observation describes what I felt, saw or measured. A hypothesis explains it and predicts what should happen under a change. Keeping these separate prevents “I feel a dip” from becoming a diagnosis of weak muscles or a particular arm fault.

## Definition and use

An observation records a sensation or visible/measured event. A hypothesis proposes a cause or mechanism.

Observation: breathing felt rushed. Alternatives: late turn, higher effort, insufficient prior exhalation, or a non-technique issue. A safe check can narrow the options but does not prove one cause from a single repeat.

## Boundary and non example

Missing video is missing information, not permission to invent a movement. Unsafe symptoms should lead to support or assessment, not deliberate reproduction as a control.
