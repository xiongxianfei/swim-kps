# Perceived effort

## Key takeaway

Perceived effort is useful personal information but not a direct measure of energy consumption.

## Summary

Perceived effort is how hard a task feels. For self-coaching, a consistent simple description can be compared alongside distance, time, breathing and execution. It helps judge manageability without equating sensation with a physiological measurement.

## Definition and use

A personal report of how demanding a particular attempt felt.

Record “easier at the same familiar distance and roughly similar pace” separately from “used less energy.” A slower task or longer rest can reduce effort without any technique improvement.

## Boundary and non example

Do not pursue maximal effort to calibrate a scale. Unusual breathlessness, dizziness, chest pain or persistent discomfort override a comparison.
