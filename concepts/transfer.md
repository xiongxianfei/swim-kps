# Transfer

## Key takeaway

Transfer asks whether a useful change survives the task it was meant to improve.

## Summary

Transfer is the continuation of a learned or rehearsed effect in another relevant task. For swimming, the key transition is often from a supported or isolated drill to ordinary whole-stroke swimming with breathing and balance.

## Definition and use

Use of an action or skill under conditions different from those in which it was practised.

After a kick rehearsal, try a short familiar whole-stroke task with normal air access. Check execution and the intended outcome; return to an easier supported task if control fails.

## Boundary and non example

One successful attempt does not establish retention on later days. This is an operational distinction, not a claim that every isolated drill helps or that a fixed number of tests proves learning.
