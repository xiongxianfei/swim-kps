# Cue

## Key takeaway

A cue helps execute an action; it is not a scientific explanation of that action.

## Summary

A cue is a brief word, image or felt intention used to direct attention. “Return long” may help organize recovery; it does not establish what forces the swimmer produced. Keep its intended effect separate from its literal wording.

## Definition and use

A short phrase or image used to help perform a chosen action.

Use one cue for a chosen attempt, then check whether the visible movement and whole task changed as intended. Record no benefit or adverse effects as readily as improvement.

## Boundary and non example

Abandon a cue that prompts breath holding, forced range, pain or loss of control. The same words can produce different movements in different people.
