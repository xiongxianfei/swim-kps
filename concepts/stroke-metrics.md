# Stroke metrics

## Key takeaway

Time, count, distance and effort answer different questions and must be interpreted together.

## Summary

Stroke metrics are observations such as swim time, stroke count, distance per counted event and breathing comfort. They are meaningful only with a defined distance, count convention and start/finish conditions.

## Definition and use

Measured distance, time and a stated count of stroke events, interpreted together.

Average speed is distance divided by time. Distance per count is distance divided by the counted events, but wall travel contributes distance without a counted stroke. Record push-off and turn differences instead of crediting all distance to stroke quality.

## Boundary and non example

A lower count can accompany a slower or harder swim. No count, ratio or combined score alone identifies metabolic efficiency, safety or durable learning.
