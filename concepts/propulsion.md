# Propulsion

## Key takeaway

Useful propulsion is water interaction contributing to the intended movement, not simply limb motion.

## Summary

Propulsion denotes the effect of fluid forces contributing to desired swimming motion. The arms and legs interact with water; the resulting whole-body motion also reflects other forces, prior speed and coordination.

## Definition and use

A useful contribution of the swimmer–water interaction to movement in the intended direction.

A board-supported kick may help reveal sequence. A fixed wall-supported kick cannot be judged by travel, because support prevents translation and adds forces. A moving whole-stroke task is needed to judge the intended application.

## Boundary and non example

Feeling pressure does not quantify useful thrust, and a stronger action can increase resistance or disrupt breathing. No universal percentage of speed is assigned to arms or legs here.
