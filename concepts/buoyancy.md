# Buoyancy

## Key takeaway

Buoyant support and the body’s orientation are different questions.

## Summary

Buoyancy is the upward resultant of fluid pressure on an immersed body. In a static uniform fluid it equals the weight of displaced fluid. Whether a swimmer is level also depends on where forces act, body configuration and motion.

## Definition and use

The upward resultant of hydrostatic pressure associated with displaced water.

A person can have enough support to float while the legs sit lower. That alone does not establish a fault. Supported pool exploration and emergency floating are different uses; neither guarantees a particular active-swimming posture.

## Boundary and non example

Do not empty the lungs, prolong submersion or force a posture to test buoyancy. Initial floating and recovery need appropriate in-person help when they are not already dependable.
